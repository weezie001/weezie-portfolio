    Fewer pairs,
    worn longer

    Stride Common started in 2019 with one running shoe and a repair bench. We still make a small range, publish the numbers behind every pair, and fix them when they wear out.

    Founded

    Why only eight models

    Most sneaker walls are the same four shoes in forty colours. We would rather make two good answers for each activity and keep every size in stock, so the pair you need is on the shelf the day you need it.

    Each model gets a year of wear testing by our own team and a panel of forty runners, players and commuters before it ships. If a pair does not earn its place, it does not get made.

    Why we resole

    The upper of a well-made shoe usually outlasts its sole by years. Our knit and leather models are cemented with a heat-release adhesive so the outsole can come off cleanly and a new one can go on.

    A resole costs $45, takes about ten working days and uses the same rubber compound as the original. It is the single thing we are proudest of.

    What we measure

    • Weight, drop and stack height for every model, measured on a US 9.
    • Fit notes written from returns data, not from the design brief.
    • Reviews collected 30 days after delivery, once the shoes have been worn.

    So far

    A better fit
    starts here

    Measure once, in the evening, in the socks you run or walk in. Then check the fit note for the model you have in mind.

    Find your size

    Heel to the tip of your longest toe, on your longer foot.

    Enter a length between 21 and 32 cm.

    Your recommended US, UK and EU sizes appear here.

    1. Stand on paper

      Heel against a wall, weight on the foot, in the socks you plan to wear.

    2. Mark heel and toe

      Hold the pen upright. Mark the back of the heel and the tip of the longest toe.

    3. Measure the longer foot

      Measure between the marks on both feet and use the larger number.

    The 60-day
    wear test

    Wear them properly: outside, on runs, on the commute. If they are not right within 60 days of delivery, return them free for a refund or a different size.

      Send your order number through the contact form. We confirm within one working day.

    Privacy
    and terms

    Plain-language summaries of the policies that apply to this store. Last updated September 2026.

    What we collect

    Your name, email, delivery address and order history, so we can deliver, handle returns and resoles, and answer your questions. Payment details are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.

    What we do not do

    • We do not sell or rent your details.
    • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
    • We only email you about marketing if you subscribe.

    Your choices

    Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at any time through the contact form. We respond within 30 days.

    Orders and prices

    Prices are in US dollars and include sales tax where it applies. An order is accepted when we send the dispatch email.

    Returns

    Pairs can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a refund or exchange, including pairs that have been worn outside, as long as they are not damaged beyond normal wear.

    Resoles

    Resoles are guaranteed against sole separation for 12 months. We will tell you before starting if an upper is too worn to resole, and return it free.
