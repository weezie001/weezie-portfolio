        About Ferrow

        A slow magazine about ordinary things done well.

        Ferrow started in 2024 at a kitchen table in Marseille, with a simple brief: find people who make the everyday more interesting, and give them the room and the photographs they deserve.

        Nine issues later we are a team of three editors and a few dozen contributors. We still travel to every story, still print on uncoated paper and still carry no advertising.

        The masthead

        Maren Solberg

        Editor in chief

        Twenty years on newspaper features desks before starting Ferrow. Edits every story twice and writes the letter.

        Kenji Arai

        Director of photography

        Shoots on medium format and commissions every photographer we publish. Has walked the coast path eleven times.

        Adaeze Okafor

        Art director

        Designs the magazine, the type and the scattered covers. Previously at a photobook studio in Lyon.

        How we work

        We go there

        Every story is reported in person, by a writer and a photographer travelling together. Nothing comes from a press release.

        We photograph what is there

        No styling teams and no props brought in. If a room is untidy, it is untidy in the magazine too.

        We print to keep

        Sewn binding, uncoated FSC paper and a printer in Verona we visit for every issue. Made to be read more than once.

        We pay contributors fairly

        Writers and photographers are paid on acceptance, at published rates, and keep the rights to their work.

        Looking after prints

        Keep out of direct sun

        Pigment inks are lightfast for decades indoors, but strong sun on a south wall will still fade the paper over time.

        Avoid damp walls

        Cotton rag paper moves with humidity. Leave a finger's width behind the frame and avoid bathrooms and kitchens.

        Handle by the border

        Unframed prints ship between boards. Lift them by the white border with clean, dry hands and frame behind glass.

        When you order or subscribe we collect your name, email address and delivery address. When you sign up for the Letter we collect your email address and how often you want to hear from us. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
