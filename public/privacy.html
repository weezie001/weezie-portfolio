Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers weezie-portfolio.vercel.app, the portfolio of Enang Weezie, a designer and full-stack developer in Abuja, Nigeria. It explains what data the site collects, why, and the choices you have.

What you send me

When you use the contact form or the project brief, you send me your name, email address, message and any project details you choose to share. The forms are delivered to my inbox through Formspree. I use this information only to reply to you and to prepare a quote, and I never sell it or share it for marketing.

Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count visits and measure page speed. They do not use cookies and do not identify you personally.

The rock-paper-scissors game

Your game progress and any discount code you win are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there when you come back. This data stays on your device and is never sent to me unless you include the code in a message.

Advertising and cookies

This site does not show ads and does not set advertising cookies. If that changes, this section will say which services are used and how to opt out.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To see, correct or delete anything you have sent me, email enangweezie@gmail.com and I will respond within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
