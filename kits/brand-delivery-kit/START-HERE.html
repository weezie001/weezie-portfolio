A good place to start.

You have the identity, the applications and the instructions. Here is how to turn them into a useful client handover.

01 / LOOK AROUND

Open the gallery.

Open index.html after extracting the ZIP. Browse 36 editable designs, then see the photographic examples. No installation or server is needed.

02 / ADD THE BRAND

Make it yours.

Choose Customize brand. Add the approved name, copy, colors, contacts and image. Start blank to remove sample photography. Save settings as a JSON file.

03 / FINISH THE ARTWORK

Export, then refine.

SVG keeps the text and shapes editable. PNG is a finished image. Replace the North sample mark in your vector editor and review every line of sample copy.

04 / HAND IT OVER

Give the client a guide.

Export the editable HTML brand book, revise the strategy and print to PDF. Deliver the agreed source files, finished exports, license notes and instructions.

What is a mockup?

A mockup is a picture showing the design in use, such as a campaign on a billboard or a logo on a tote. The supplied JPGs are finished presentation examples. They are not layered PSD files or 3D models. The matching flat artwork is editable SVG.

The brand editor updates the SVG layouts and exported HTML book. It does not rebrand the static photographic JPGs or the supplied PDF samples. Open the production guide before placing a print order.

What belongs in the final client folder?

Approved logo variants, approved guidelines PDF, the applications agreed in your scope, ready-to-use image exports, source files, font/license notes and a short usage guide. Remove unused sample designs. Agree specialist needs such as packaging dielines, animation or vehicle graphics separately.
