# Page library

The page types a Scattered-Cards Magazine site needs, in the order a reader
meets them. Section names match the template. Adapt the content, keep the
composition. The complete route table of the worked example is in
`route-inventory.md`; a buyer's site renames, adds or drops pages to fit its
brief (see "Adapting to a real brief" in SKILL.md), keeping Home, a story
collection with article pages, topics, a form page and the not-found page.

## Home

1. **Editorial hero**: centred eyebrow (publication line and issue), a fan
   of five scattered story cards with one-word topic captions, a two-line
   heavy serif headline, a two-sentence lead and one outlined CTA (find your
   next story). Nothing else above the fold.
2. **Topic index**: sticky aside (h2, one paragraph, link to all topics) and
   the ruled list of topics with counts.
3. **New in the current issue**: 7/5 grid, portrait lead card left, two
   landscape cards stacked right.
4. **Feature**: one full-bleed landscape photograph with an offset two-column
   caption (kicker, h2 / dek, link).
5. **More to read**: portrait cards in a horizontal carousel with buttons,
   swipe and arrow keys.
6. **Issue band**: tinted band, composed cover over the previous cover, title,
   lead, table of contents with page numbers and "Read online" links, price,
   add to bag, subscription link.
7. **How an issue is made**: four ruled stats that count up once.
8. **Letters from readers**: three scattered paper notes with name and place.
9. **Newsletter band**: bordered panel with email field and promise.

## Stories (collection)

Breadcrumb, h1 and lead; sticky toolbar with search and topic chips (plus
"Saved" once a story is saved); result line with count, clear filters, sort
and grid or index toggle; asymmetric 7/5 grid or ruled index list; empty state
with suggestions and two actions.

## Story (article)

Breadcrumb to stories and topic. Landscape lead: centred head (kicker with
topic, issue and date; title; italic dek; byline; save and copy link), then a
wide 16:9 photograph with caption. Portrait lead: 5/7 split with the photo
left and the head right. Then a 3/7/2 grid: sticky facts aside (four facts,
credits) and the serif body with drop cap, subheads, pull quote and detail
figure; an end row (filed under, buy the issue). Then the next story panel
and "More on [topic]" with other online stories and the print archive.

## Topics and topic

Topics index: the full ruled index with permanent thumbnails, long
descriptions and counts. Topic page: giant topic name beside the intro and
three figures (online, in print, issues); "Read online" (a single row card or
a 7/5 grid); "In print" archive rows with issue, page and buy or sold-out;
other topics as chips.

## Shop and product

Shop: h1 with lead beside a delivery note box; sticky category chips; count
and sort; three-column product stages (composed covers on tint, framed prints,
full-bleed notebook photo), stock badge, quick add; subscription band with a
fan of covers. Product: sticky gallery with views (cover, spread built from a
real story, detail zoom; or framed preview that follows the options), info
column with kicker, h1, live price and stock, lead, option pills, quantity,
add to bag with loading and done states, assurances, compact accordion
(details with specs, delivery, from the story); issue contents; related items.

## Bag, checkout, subscribe

Bag drawer (from the header and after adding on a product page) and bag page
(lines, delivery zone, totals, checkout). Checkout: numbered fieldsets, live
delivery pricing by country and method, summary first on mobile behind a
toggle, inline errors, honest confirmation. Subscribe: hero with cover fan,
segmented controls for recipient and term, two plan cards with per-issue
price, gift details form validated before adding, FAQ.

## About, contact and utilities

About: statement h1, two-column intro, masthead portraits with bios,
principles with icons, stockists list, pitch band. Contact: aside with
copyable addresses and reply time; tabs with three validated forms. FAQ:
category nav, search, grouped accordions, empty state. Newsletter: form with
frequency and a sample letter on rotated paper. Delivery: rate table, care
steps, returns. Privacy and terms: sticky contents and short real sections.
404: numeral, sentence, actions, three suggested story cards in a small fan.
