    Only wake someone when it matters

    Most pages are noise: a flapping check, twelve alerts for one root cause, a SEV-4 at 3 a.m. Pulsegrid filters them before they reach a phone.

    • Flap detection

      Checks that bounce between up and down are held until they settle, then reported once.

    • One incident per root cause

      Alerts from the same service, region and minute are grouped into a single incident with a single page.

    • Quiet hours by severity

      Low-severity alerts wait for business hours. A SEV-1 still gets through, every time.
