# Interior page quality

Every inner page must be useful on its own. A route count is not a quality
measure; depth comes from structure, working tools and distinct imagery.

- **Product pages**: gallery with more than one view, variant selectors that
  change price or availability, a size grid with real stock states, a delivery
  estimate, materials, fit and care, spec tiles, reviews and related items.
  Never repeat the card description as the body.
- **Collection pages**: their own context photo, the models in the collection,
  an aid to choose between them and activity-specific buying advice.
- **Help pages**: a working tool or a clear decision aid (size finder and
  chart, shipping options, return steps, FAQ by topic). Explain what the action
  does and what it does not do in the demo.
- **Company pages**: a narrative with a point of view, real numbers for the
  fictional brand, a timeline or story blocks, one relevant image.
- **Forms**: only the fields that help (topic-dependent fields appear when
  needed), inline validation, a success state that states plainly that nothing
  was sent or charged in the preview.
- **Utility pages**: saved items, legal tabs and a designed 404 with a way
  forward.

Reuse the template's typography, palette, cards and controls. Do not repeat the
home hero on inner pages. Keep every page responsive and keyboard accessible.
