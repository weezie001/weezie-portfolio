Dear fal Design Team,

I am applying for the Senior Product Designer role at fal. I have worked in design since 2023 and expanded into full-stack development in 2025. My strength is carrying a product idea from user flows, sitemaps and Figma prototypes into a coherent interface and working implementation. fal's focus on generative media is especially compelling to me because I have already worked on the practical design questions that arise when an interface depends on an image-generation model.

For HEIS KITS, I designed and built shopping experiences that include an AI virtual try-on workflow. That meant thinking beyond the generated image: helping customers upload a suitable photo, confirm a size, understand generation progress and usage limits, recover from errors, and download a result. The implementation also required attention to caching and the tradeoffs between model quality and response time. I would bring that combination of interaction design and technical understanding to fal's tools for creators and developers.

ARTEEC demonstrates a different part of my practice: connecting product direction to a reusable design system. The art-marketplace and creative-community pilot includes a coverage map linking 33 original wireframes to components across artist, buyer, organiser and moderation journeys. I implemented mobile bottom navigation and overflow sheets alongside desktop patterns, and developed connected submission, review, profile and enquiry workflows. My Weezie UI library extends that systems work into 35 React components with usage examples, tokens and integration guidance.

I also design the operational workflows behind the interface. My portfolio includes PadUp's storefront-to-office payment notifications, CRM outreach and incoming-email automation, Portfolio Doctor's automated reporting prototype, and BotTask Escrow's testnet task-settlement interface. These projects have taught me to make system states, dependencies and next actions understandable to the person using the product.

I lead ideation, client onboarding, design and product decisions in my independent work, and can carry those decisions into code and handoff documentation. I would welcome the opportunity to bring this hands-on approach to fal, collaborate closely with product and engineering, and refine designs through customer feedback. I recently completed my B.Tech in Urban & Regional Planning at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, and am open to relocating from Abuja to San Francisco.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be glad to walk you through the decisions, constraints and implementation behind these projects.
